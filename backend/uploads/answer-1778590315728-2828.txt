1.Photosynthesis is the process by which plants make food. It uses sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide. Green leaves contain chlorophyll for this process. Oxygen is released during photosynthesis. It is important for plants and humans.

2.The heart is a muscular organ in the body. It pumps blood to all body parts. The heart has four chambers. It helps in blood circulation. Oxygen is supplied through blood.

3.Chromosomes are present inside the nucleus. They contain genetic information. They help transfer characters from parents to children. Humans have 46 chromosomes. They are important for heredity.

4.Respiration is the process of releasing energy from food. Oxygen is used in aerobic respiration. Carbon dioxide is released. Respiration occurs in cells. It is necessary for survival.

5.Cell is the basic unit of life. All living things are made of cells. Cells perform important functions in the body. Different cells do different works. Cells help in growth and repair.
